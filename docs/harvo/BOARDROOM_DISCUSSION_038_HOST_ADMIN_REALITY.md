## Business thesis to test

Encho's primary buyer is the independent host who needs a credible property web presence, direct demand, fast inquiry handling and more fulfilled contribution after acquisition costs. A host should not need Meta/Google OAuth or an advertising specialist. Admin and scoped staff should turn reusable expert policies into safe, observable operations rather than manually assemble every campaign. Guests still need truthful room offers and a working, legally cleared purchase path. Ads without an available and bookable offer are expenditure, not a business moat.

The currently intended commercial separation is Flex's default 15% booking commission or Growth's ₹4,999 + GST per-property rolling 30-day subscription/0% commission while active, versus optional advertising at recognized campaign cost `C` plus prospective markup `p` (introductory 3–5% intent). The precise recognized cost, tax, overage, refunds and fee earning rules remain open. Historical fixed 15% ad fees and simulated pilot economics must not be used as current promises.

## Critical handoffs where the business can fail

1. **Property release → promoted truth:** a host may request publication after approved-photo minima, while the UI implies review. Set one enforceable policy across listing, guest page and creative approval. A materially edited room price/media/amenity must not keep stale approval.
2. **Resort → exact offer:** one property may have budget, comfort and premium rooms. Resolve an explicit room offer, dated price/availability, occupancy, cancellation terms and landing representation before assigning a tier or funding an ad. A “starts from” property ad is a distinct discovery product and must truthfully show the starting offer.
3. **Host preference → expert strategy:** host selects business outcome, exact offer, allowed media format, dates/budget and bounded feeder preference; strategy owns objective, attribution, bidding, conversion event, exclusions and limits. Deterministic checks reject unsafe combinations; AI proposes corrections but cannot approve or spend.
4. **Human review → funding → provider:** approve exact revision, obtain accepted itemized quote, verify capture and risk release, compile saved strategy, create paused, read back, then activate under authorized state. No manual “mark paid” shortcut.
5. **Campaign → inquiry → fulfilled economics:** each flight needs its own provider/status/spend evidence; one guest booking must not be counted multiple times across concurrent campaigns. Event source, consent, attribution rule, confidence/freshness and refund/cancellation status must be visible. Today guest checkout closure prevents certifying this loop.
6. **Lead → response:** a campaign that produces inquiries while host notifications or staff escalation fail destroys paid value. Prioritize durable notification receipts, inbox assignment, response SLA and transparent handoff over visual animation.

## External product research and bounded lessons

- [Etsy Offsite Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work) exposes listing/search-term traffic, attributed orders and fees to sellers. Its success-fee model differs from Encho's proposed cost-plus funding; copy **outcome and charge transparency**, not its commercial terms or attribution rule without a decision.
- [Evocalize](https://evocalize.com/) presents a central marketing team defining brand/strategy with localized execution across distributed businesses. Its claims are vendor descriptions, not Encho evidence. The useful pattern is versioned centrally released programs with bounded local choice.
- [Sojern's hospitality campaign reporting](https://portalsupport.sojern.com/hc/en-us/articles/27639333288212-Sojern-Reporting-Types) distinguishes managed campaign performance, conversions and revenue; its [pricing page](https://www.sojern.com/pricing) separates commission and managed-campaign models. Encho should distinguish media spend, Encho fee, inquiry, confirmed/fulfilled booking and reconciliation in one host view, and measure whether 3–5% markup can pay for human operations.
- [Hostaway's communication product](https://www.hostaway.com/features/communication) centers guest messaging, AI replies and automation. This is a vendor feature description. The lesson is an assigned, measurable reply workflow; Encho must still prove authenticated delivery and host/staff authority before claiming a unified service operation.
- [Google's third-party policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6086450?hl=en) says separate accounts for end advertisers matter. Zero Host OAuth can coexist with an Encho-managed account structure, but using one shared serving account for unrelated hosts is not a safe assumption. Meta topology also needs current provider-specific evidence.

## Proposed improvements for discussion, not approved decisions

**P1 — One truthful vertical slice.** Choose one existing property, one specific room/offer, one approved image or video, one account-supported Meta or Google program, one funded paused publication and one inquiry/booking reconciliation path. Pass actual restricted-role, provider-readback and source/artifact/schema checks before adding broader controls. This tests the moat rather than the number of controls.

**P2 — Host intent screen.** Default to property + exact offer + goal + approved creative + total investment + dates. Present an optional advanced panel for approved feeder markets, supported placements/format preferences and guest-fit intent. Every preference needs an explicit compatibility result; no direct raw relationship-demographic targeting without demonstrated provider capability and policy basis. Show “Encho recommends X because…” with editable bounds and a request-for-expert-review escape path. Preserve separate independent consent to charge and to materially change a campaign.

**P3 — Expert program studio.** Build on existing AdTech releases: price **per offer**, trip intent and corridor become composable policy dimensions. Provide effective-plan preview, provider account/capability matrix, expected cost and reach assumptions with uncertainty, exact changed fields, canary status, approval requirements and rollback. A saved strategy never retroactively changes funded revisions. Avoid dozens of undifferentiated native API toggles.

**P4 — Admin exception desk.** Prioritize pending approval, stale observation, provider rejection, spend risk, unavailable inventory, overdue inquiry and customer disputes; assign an accountable owner and SLA per item. The expert configures reusable policy once, reviews risky exceptions and can issue bounded audited post-publication commands with readback. Finance and campaign approver roles stay separated where risk requires it.

**P5 — Campaign and service ledger visible to the host.** Show each simultaneous flight separately and a deduplicated portfolio total. Label requested/confirmed provider state, report freshness, charged/captured/reserved/refundable funds, attributable inquiries, and confirmed versus fulfilled bookings. Send actionable inbox alerts with delivery receipts and an escalation route. The budget meter must use observed spend and must never suggest unknown = zero or automatic refunds.

**P6 — Release integrity first.** Recover missing applied migration bytes and actual deployed artifact identity, use restricted non-BYPASSRLS runtime logins, obtain real legal/provider/commercial authority, and independently reaccept original packages. Generated simulation receipts remain quarantined. No “10/10” or production-ready declaration can substitute for these proofs.

## Decisions needed from the founder before this becomes a new blueprint

1. **Listing release:** mandatory human Admin release for first publication and material edits (recommended initially), or host self-publication after individually approved facts/media? Both require one consistent UI/API rule and an emergency unpublish path.
2. **First paid pilot offer:** which *specific room or entire-stay offer* and destination should be the narrow pilot? A resort-wide “starts from” discovery campaign and a room-specific booking campaign have different truth/attribution requirements.
3. **Host control budget:** which preferences are truly essential at launch—format (Feed/Reel/Stories), approved feeder locations, guest-fit intent, and/or age request? Recommendation: format + feeder preference + evidence-backed guest-fit, with age treated as expert policy rather than a host execution control until provider-policy review.
4. **Operating promise:** what human review hours and first-response SLA can Encho staff in reality? Do not advertise a five-minute response or unlimited concierge service without staffing and alert delivery proof.
5. **Provider commercial identity:** who is the advertiser of record for each property's ads, and what account topology has provider-specific approval? The host should remain free of provider OAuth, but the backend serving structure cannot be guessed.

## Boardroom verdict

The project has a thoughtful architecture and real code for listing, review, finance, policy and telemetry. It is **not** currently independently certified as an industrial production operation. The largest problem is not a missing animated dashboard; it is an unproven offer-to-booking loop and incomplete release/account/workforce evidence. Improve the operating chain and make its status legible to hosts and staff. Retain this note as discussion; promote a choice to `DECISIONS.md` only after the founder explicitly makes it.
