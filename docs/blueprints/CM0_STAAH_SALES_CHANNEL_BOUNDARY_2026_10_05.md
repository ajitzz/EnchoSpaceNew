# CM-0 — Encho as a STAAH-connected sales channel

**Status:** design checkpoint, not an integration or certification claim. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-05. Public source: [STAAH ChannelConnect v2](https://getapidoc.staah.net/v2/) and its [root documentation](https://getapidoc.staah.net/), which currently presents v2. Its visible change log includes an August 2024 reservation `extraData` entry; the public page does not establish Encho's partner contract, enabled capabilities or live credentials. This design preserves W0 public truth, W1 accepted revisions and W2 quote/temporary-hold authority. No provider call, production migration, reservation confirmation or payment is authorized here.

**Direction:** Encho is the OTA/sales channel; STAAH is the upstream channel manager for a consenting property. STAAH's documented push of rates, room-level inventory and rate-plan restrictions supplies observations. Encho's accepted commercial offer and temporary local hold remain separate authorities. Before a *future* confirmation, Encho must make the documented live rates/availability/restrictions call and durably deliver the reservation to STAAH. A local hold is neither a global lock nor a confirmed upstream reservation. STAAH documentation says mandatory push, mapping and reservation APIs; partner IDs are used for room, rate and reservation; an HTTP 200 can contain `status: fail`. It does not imply Encho receives bookings made on other OTAs.

**First-release design limit:** one explicitly mapped manageable rate plan per canonical room, room-based INR rate only, and only restriction semantics Encho can represent and verify exactly. This is a proposed engineering safety limit, not approved commercial policy or proof STAAH accepts it. Other mapped plans and unrecognized occupancy/tax/restriction combinations remain closed. STAAH describes room-level inventory but rate-plan-level restrictions. A future multi-plan W1 change must add rate-plan identity without collapsing same-room offers; manual successor review cannot be bypassed by a push unless a bounded delegated acceptance policy is approved. The current W1 overlap guard therefore stays intact.

**Partnership enquiry packet — prepared, not sent:** Ask STAAH for (1) channel partner agreement and certification checklist; (2) supported Encho-as-OTA topology, one-plan-per-room pilot acceptability, test property and mapping ownership; (3) exact v2 schema/capability version, inclusive/exclusive tax and occupancy-pricing choices; (4) push retry/order/`trackingId` guarantees and outage replay; (5) live ARR limits and confirmation timing; (6) reservation amendment/cancel and unknown-outcome lookup contract; (7) per-property key scope, endpoint/allowlist/static-egress requirements and rotation; (8) sandbox and production credentials only through approved secure exchange. Do not request or transmit guest data, credentials or live reservations in an enquiry.

**Deferred:** Encho acting as a channel manager for other OTAs, receiving unrelated OTA private reservations, payment/capture, automated acceptance of upstream commercial terms and a global inventory lock. None follows from ChannelConnect public documentation.
