# Meta Creative & Ad Policy
## Image/Video Specs
- Formats: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9.
- Text on Image: Recommended to be less than 20% to avoid delivery penalties.
- Quality: No blur, low resolution, or compression artifacts.
- No misleading buttons (e.g., a "play" button on a static image).
- No deceptive before-and-after imagery.

## Copy Rules
- No profanity or discriminatory language.
- Do not assert or imply personal attributes (e.g., "Are you a single mom?").
- No unrealistic promises (e.g., "Guaranteed to double your money").
- Ensure landing page content matches ad creative.

## Landing Page
- Must not lead to error pages (404, 500).
- No excessive pop-ups or auto-playing sounds.
- Content must match the ad's offer.
