# Meta Housing Policy (Special Ad Category)
Ads for real estate, housing, and related services must comply with the Housing Special Ad Category (HEC).

## Strict Rules
- Age targeting must be 18-65+ (cannot narrow).
- Gender targeting must include all genders.
- Location targeting must have a minimum radius of 15 miles (25 kilometers) around any specific address or pin drop.
- Exclusions based on demographic categories, behaviors, or specific ZIP codes are not allowed.
- Discriminatory language or imagery is strictly prohibited.
- "Lookalike" audiences are replaced with "Special Ad Audiences" which have restricted matching.

## Impact on Encho
All Encho campaigns fall under HEC. The preflight engine and UI must strictly enforce these targeting minimums.
