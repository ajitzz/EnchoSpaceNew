# Meta Landing Page Policy & Destination Standards
- Ensure the landing page is fully functional (No 404s, 500s).
- Must use HTTPS.
- Cannot contain deceptive practices (e.g., misleading offers, fake reviews).
- Content on the landing page must strictly match the ad's creative and offer.
- Must have clear contact information or a Privacy Policy if collecting leads.
- Cannot feature pop-ups or interstitial ads that block content.
