# W1 accepted dated sellable offer — implementation impact

**Base:** `dbe20c6a1b318bb5ddee2a5d33adaa536c44c212`. W0 remains locally accepted and immutable. This is a local engineering scope, not legal checkout or deployed acceptance.

**Existing authority:** `room_types.id` identifies a canonical room; `inventory_days` records dated capacity; approved `media_assets` and published `listings` supply public truth. `CanonicalRoomOfferReader` is preflight evidence only. Legacy `offers`, `calendar_prices`, `listings.price`, `room_types.base_price`, test-commerce quotes and marketing snapshots are not accepted sale authority.

**Source of truth to add:** One provider-independent sellable offer identity with numbered, immutable commercial revisions and a durable explicit Host submission followed by exact scoped staff acceptance. The amount is INR paise **per room night** for a half-open stay-date scope, with an effective-time scope, occupancy/minimum-night conditions, source-fact/media fingerprints and current inventory context. It is an informational accepted offer price, not a dated booking quote, tax-inclusive total, reservation or availability guarantee. An undated public `Rooms from` summary may select only an eligible accepted revision with at least one currently open qualifying stay; it must expose the applicable date context. No eligible accepted offer retains W0's null price and `VERIFIED_OFFER_UNAVAILABLE` state.

**Affected surfaces:** Narrow additive migration 049; new offer contract/service and real PostgreSQL tests; mounted Host draft/submit and staff review/accept/retire routes; Host and Admin controls; the shared Guest public stay resolver, catalogue/card/map/detail/refresh/SEO projections and clients. Existing inventory hold/commerce and campaign workers remain consumers of future offer identity, not modified payment or ad-spend paths.

**Security and compatibility:** Host identity comes from persisted session and room ownership; staff identity/permission from restricted workforce IAM. Parent listing and exact revision are locked and revalidated under one transaction. Foreign rooms, stale facts/media, expired dates, unpublished properties and missing inventory fail closed. Accepted commercial bytes cannot be updated or deleted; successor acceptance changes public selection without rewriting history. Existing W0 no-offer behavior and URLs remain compatible. No old SQL migration bytes change and migration 049 is tested only on disposable PostgreSQL.

**Validation and rollback:** Failing-before mounted/real-PG fixture; lifecycle, stale-review, concurrency, RLS separate-login and direct-mutation negatives; same-class two-room public parity; targeted Host/Admin/Guest UI tests; Node 24 typecheck/lint/offline build; built desktop/360px journey; frozen independent review. Rollback disables new offer commands/public accepted-price selection while preserving rows, history and W0 withheld-price behavior. It never restores legacy price fallback or performs remote migration/payment/provider actions.
