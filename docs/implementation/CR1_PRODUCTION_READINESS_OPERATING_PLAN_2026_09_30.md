## Product outcome and release boundary

The first complete release must prove one connected Guest–Host–Staff journey: a host publishes a truthful, approved sellable offer; an eligible guest sees the same offer and can inquire or, when approved commerce policy permits, book it; scoped staff review the exact campaign revision; accepted money and risk controls precede paused provider creation; authenticated readback precedes activation; the host sees each campaign's observed state, dated spend and canonical inquiry/booking outcomes. Requested provider actions, browser refreshes and payment redirects are not evidence of completed remote or financial effects.

Preserve the canonical room/inventory, finance journal, durable outbox, conversation, marketing-v2, AdTech release, workforce IAM and migration authorities. Add narrow adapters or commands; do not create a second wallet, approval path, booking authority or provider truth store. The historical 48/48 claim is disputed; the current registers report **0/32 full corrective cards accepted** and **0/48 original packages independently reaccepted**. These are acceptance counts, not code-completion percentages.

The older `ENCHO_BUSINESS_PLAN_AND_STRATEGY.md` still describes a fixed 15% advertising fee, automatic contact masking and one universal Encho serving account. Those are historical proposals, not current execution authority. The Constitution instead records cost-plus markup direction with unresolved tax/cost/refund rules, disclosed CRM policy and provider-specific account topology. Product copy, finance tests and provider compilers must follow the latter boundary until an approved decision changes it.

Pilot measurement should record eligible host draft-to-review time, revision/rejection reasons, confirmed paused readback rate, minutes since each provider observation, spend between safety-pause request and confirmed remote pause, inquiry delivery and first-response distribution, inquiry-to-captured-booking and booking-to-fulfilled-stay conversion, refunds/chargebacks and true contribution after provider spend, gateway cost, tax and staff work. Set numeric targets only after a measured baseline and operating owner approve them; attractive UI or click volume alone does not prove host value.
